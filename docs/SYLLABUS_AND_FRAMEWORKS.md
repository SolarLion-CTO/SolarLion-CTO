## 2. Twenty key concepts for strategic AI decision-making (as listed)

| # | Concept | Decision-making application |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mintzberg 5Ps | Use multiple lenses to shape AI strategy (plan, position, pattern, perspective, ploy) |
| 2 | Porter's generic strategies | Choose cost leadership, differentiation or focus strategy for AI initiatives |
| 3 | Theory of Constraints | Identify and address the real constraint limiting AI performance or scale |
| 4 | Strategy quality tests | Test strategy for consistency, consonance, advantage and feasibility |
| 5 | Pipeline vs platform | Decide whether AI is a workflow (pipeline) or a reusable enterprise capability (platform) |
| 6 | Open vs closed architecture | Choose between provider-managed (closed) and open-weight / self-hosted (open) |
| 7 | Investment matrix | Prioritise AI initiatives based on value, risk and investment |
| 8 | Risk process | Identify, assess, intervene and govern AI risks continuously |
| 9 | Stage-gate | Use gates to control progression from idea → MVP → production → scale |
| 10 | Crossing the chasm | Navigate AI adoption from innovators to the mainstream enterprise market |
| 11 | Diffusion factors | Evaluate relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, trialability and observability |
| 12 | Network effects | Leverage data, users, agents and ecosystem advantages to create compounding value |
| 13 | Modularity × coordination | Decide between platform, ecosystem or vertically integrated architecture |
| 14 | McKinsey 7S | Align seven dimensions: strategy, structure, systems, skills, staff, style and shared values |
| 15 | Kotter 8 steps | Drive change through an 8-step enterprise transformation approach |
| 16 | Lewin 3 stages | Manage change in three stages: unfreeze → change → refreeze |
| 17 | Theory E vs Theory O | Balance economic transformation (Theory E) with organisational capability (Theory O) |
| 18 | Business Model Canvas | Define AI value proposition, customers, channels, key partners and revenue model |
| 19 | XaaS | Consume or deliver AI, platform and model capabilities as a service |
| 20 | Winner-take-all | Assess whether network / ecosystem economics favour concentration and scale leadership |

(The source image numbers "Theory E vs O" as 18 by a typo; it is concept 17 here.)
